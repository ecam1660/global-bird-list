Global Bird List: Subspecies tab, from birddata.csv

Subspecies tab, built from your birddata.csv. These tiles use Option A (Scale to fit) and stay 1 × 3. They open on the Subspecies tab; the Overview tab is unchanged.
  • Count: the circle on the tab is # SSP. Extinct subspecies are counted.
  • Names: with an SSP NAME, the row reads "South I. (australis)"; without one, it shows just the scientific name.
  • Monotypic subspecies get an Ⓜ. Polytypic ones are grouped under their group name with a Ⓟ, and the group's subspecies sit indented below it.
  • Group check box: it takes the best state of its subspecies, where ✓ beats ?. Click a subspecies box to cycle blank → ✓ → ?, and the group updates.
  • Extinct subspecies (IUCN = EX) get a crossed-out box and an EX pill. Their box can't be checked.
  • Links: a ✓ subspecies with a LINK gets a clickable check box with a small ↗ (try Stewart I. on Southern Brown Kiwi). It opens the observation in a new tab. Only ✓ rows link.
  • Range: SHORT RANGE. All 5,768 subspecies of your lifers are 45 characters or fewer, so they fit on one line.
Everything here is your real data: subspecies, names, types, ✓/? states, ranges and countries. The photo and audio icons are off until those columns return.
